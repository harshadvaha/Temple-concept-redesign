BrainFlow

Lie down and your pulse slows — but blood flow to your brain rises. A wrist sees only the drop. In early testing, BrainFlow followed the rise, tracking transcranial Doppler at r≈0.8.

Entropy

{{entropy}} / 250 · simulated

A 1–250 score, updated every second, for how hard your body is working to shed heat. Your low at rest is your Baseline; the highest you sustain near max heart rate is your Threshold. Together they make Momentum.

Dashed lines: illustrative Baseline and Threshold. Values are simulated.
