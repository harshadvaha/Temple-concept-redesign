01 / Gravity

Every hour you stand,
your brain works uphill.

Your brain burns a fifth of your body’s oxygen, and sits at the top of your circulation. Temple began as a question about what gravity does to that supply. Tilt the body and see the idea.

Estimated brain blood flow vs lying flat

{{flowLabel}}

Illustrates the “Gravity Ageing” hypothesis from Temple’s research arm, Continue: that upright posture can lower brain blood flow by up to 17%. It is still being tested, and some clinicians dispute it. BrainFlow lets you watch your own response instead of taking anyone’s word for it.
